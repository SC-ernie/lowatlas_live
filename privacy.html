Privacy

No cookies. No trackers.

Last updated November 13, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't use cookies, analytics, advertising pixels, or third-party scripts. It has no accounts and no forms, and it doesn't track you.

What this site collects

Nothing, through its own code. There is no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is stored in your browser. The free presence check runs entirely in your browser: your answers aren't sent or saved anywhere. If you press "Send results to Lowatlas," your email app opens with a draft, and nothing is sent until you send it yourself.

If you email me

I receive whatever you write, such as your name, email address, and details about your business. I use it only to reply to you and, if you hire me, to do the work. I don't sell it or share it for advertising.

Hosting and security providers

This site is delivered through hosting and security providers (currently GitHub Pages and Cloudflare). Like any website host, they automatically process visitors' IP addresses and basic request details to deliver pages and block attacks. I only see aggregate traffic numbers, not profiles of individual visitors.

Images from other sites

A few concept-project photos are loaded from Unsplash. When they load, Unsplash can see your IP address and browser details, like any image hosted on any site. Your browser is told not to send the address of this page along with the request.

Cookies and Do Not Track

This site sets no cookies. Because it doesn't track visitors, it behaves the same whether or not your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal.

Client information

If we work together, you may share business details, photos, and manager access to your profiles or ad accounts. I use that only for your project. I ask for manager access, never your passwords, and you can remove my access at any time.

Deleting your information

If you've emailed me and want your information deleted, write to lowatlas.studio@gmail.com and I'll delete it.

If this changes

If I ever add analytics or advertising tools to this site, I'll update this page and ask for your consent before they load.
